Worth knowing

Clear answers. No fine print.

Built for private-game recordkeeping. Not a poker platform or payment processor.

Does everyone have to sign up?

No. The host signs in to manage the game. Guests can open a shared read-only link for the figures available to them. A shared link is not proof of the viewer's identity.

Can a player change the numbers?

No. Guest links are read-only. Only the authorised host manages the table and its records.

Does Holdings send or receive money?

No. Holdings records financial figures and proposes who should pay whom. It never holds, sends or receives player funds.

What do signatures mean?

Players sign on screen to acknowledge recorded buy-ins and cash-outs. A signature records an acknowledgment, not proof of legal identity or confirmation that funds actually changed hands.

Does it work offline?

Holdings is currently designed around an online web app. Some signed transactions can be queued during a connection interruption in the preview, but complete offline operation is not yet supported.

Can I use Holdings on my phone?

Yes, the web app is mobile-friendly. Dedicated App Store and Google Play downloads are not yet available.
