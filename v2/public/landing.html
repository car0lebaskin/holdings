A few things worth knowing

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Does everyone need to sign up?

No. The host uses an account to manage the table. Players can open a shared read-only link to view the figures available to them.

Can players change the buy-ins?

No. Shared guest views are read-only. The host manages player seating, transactions and game closure.

Does Holdings handle payments?

No. Holdings tracks transactions and works out settlement figures. It does not move or hold player funds.

Can I use Holdings on my phone?

Yes. The web app is designed for mobile use. App Store and Google Play downloads are not available yet.

Can I look back at old sessions?

Yes. Your game history and player statistics are available from the host dashboard.
